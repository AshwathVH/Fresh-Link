About Green Trade Network

Green Trade Network is a lightweight marketplace platform designed to let farmers list produce directly for local buyers. We focus on transparency, local economies, and reducing food miles. Farmers can add listings with product details, harvest dates, prices, location and photos. Buyers can filter by location and category, view farmer profiles and contact them directly.

Why Green Trade Network?

  • 🌱 Fair prices by eliminating middlemen
  • 🌍 Local consumption reduces carbon footprint
  • 📱 Easy-to-use listing tools for farmers
  • 📍 Location-based discovery for buyers
  • 🤝 Direct connection between producers and consumers

Frequently Asked Questions

💰 What does Green Trade Network charge?
Green Trade Network is completely free to use. We charge zero listing fees, no commissions, and no hidden charges. Our mission is to support farmers and local communities.
💳 How do buyers pay farmers?
Green Trade Network does not process payments. Buyers and farmers arrange payment directly using their preferred method – cash, UPI, bank transfer, or any other mutually agreed method.
✅ Is there verification for farmers?
Yes, farmers must register and login to create listings. This ensures accountability and builds trust between buyers and sellers.
📍 How does location-based search work?
Farmers add their location when creating listings. Buyers can search and filter products based on their area to find the nearest farmers and freshest local produce.
🚚 Does Green Trade Network handle delivery?
No, Green Trade Network is a listing platform. Farmers and buyers arrange pickup or delivery directly between themselves based on mutual agreement.
