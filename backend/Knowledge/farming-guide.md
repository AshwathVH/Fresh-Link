# Indian Farming Knowledge Base

## Crop Calendar - Major Crops

### Kharif Season (June-October, Monsoon)
- **Rice**: Transplant in June-July, harvest in October-November
- **Cotton**: Sow in May-June, harvest in October-December
- **Maize**: Sow in June-July, harvest in September-October
- **Sugarcane**: Plant in February-March, harvest after 12-18 months
- **Groundnut**: Sow in June-July, harvest in October
- **Soybean**: Sow in June-July, harvest in September-October

### Rabi Season (October-March, Winter)
- **Wheat**: Sow in October-November, harvest in March-April
- **Chickpea (Chana)**: Sow in October-November, harvest in February-March
- **Mustard**: Sow in October, harvest in February-March
- **Potato**: Plant in October, harvest in January-February
- **Peas**: Sow in October-November, harvest in January-February

### Zaid Season (March-June, Summer)
- **Watermelon**: Sow in February-March, harvest in May-June
- **Cucumber**: Sow in February-March
- **Vegetables**: Bitter gourd, bottle gourd, pumpkin

## Soil Types and Suitable Crops

### Black Soil (Regur)
- Best for: Cotton, sugarcane, wheat, jowar, linseed
- Found in: Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh

### Alluvial Soil
- Best for: Rice, wheat, sugarcane, vegetables
- Found in: Indo-Gangetic plains, Punjab, Haryana, UP

### Red Soil
- Best for: Groundnut, tobacco, millets, pulses
- Found in: Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh

### Laterite Soil
- Best for: Tea, coffee, rubber, coconut, cashew
- Found in: Kerala, Karnataka coast, Northeast

## Irrigation Methods

### Drip Irrigation
- Saves 40-60% water compared to flood irrigation
- Best for: vegetables, fruits, cotton
- Initial cost: ₹30,000-50,000 per acre
- Government subsidy available: 50-90%

### Sprinkler Irrigation
- Saves 30-40% water
- Best for: wheat, pulses, vegetables
- Cost: ₹20,000-35,000 per acre

### Flood Irrigation
- Traditional method, high water use
- Suitable for rice paddy

## Organic Pest Control

### Neem-Based Solutions
- Neem oil spray: 5ml per liter of water
- Effective against aphids, whiteflies, caterpillars
- Apply early morning or evening

### Companion Planting
- Marigold with vegetables - deters nematodes
- Basil with tomatoes - repels aphids
- Onion/garlic with most crops - natural pest deterrent

### Biological Control
- Trichogramma cards for stem borers
- Ladybugs for aphid control
- Trichoderma for soil-borne diseases

## Fertilizer Guidelines

### NPK Requirements (per acre)
- Rice: N-120kg, P-60kg, K-60kg
- Wheat: N-120kg, P-60kg, K-40kg
- Cotton: N-60kg, P-30kg, K-30kg

### Organic Alternatives
- Vermicompost: 2-4 tonnes per acre
- Farm Yard Manure (FYM): 5-10 tonnes per acre
- Green manure: Dhaincha, Sesbania

## Government Schemes

### PM-KISAN
- ₹6,000 per year to farmers
- Paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000

### Soil Health Card
- Free soil testing
- Fertilizer recommendations
- Apply at nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra

### Crop Insurance (PMFBY)
- Premium: 1.5-5% of sum insured
- Coverage: Natural calamities, pests, diseases

## Market Price References (General)
- Wheat MSP: ~₹2,200 per quintal
- Rice MSP: ~₹2,100 per quintal
- Cotton MSP: ~₹6,600 per quintal
- Soybean MSP: ~₹4,600 per quintal

*Note: MSP (Minimum Support Price) changes annually*
